# Public Claims

## SAFE_PUBLIC_CLAIMS

- “This synthetic A/B demonstration estimated an observed treatment-control conversion difference of +0.86 percentage points.”
- “The 95% Newcombe confidence interval ranged from approximately -0.31 to +2.03 percentage points and included zero.”
- “The demonstration did not establish a treatment improvement at the 5% significance level.”
- “The observed point estimate was below the scenario +1.0-point practical threshold, while the interval included values both below and above it.”
- “Relative lift was +9.0717% as a secondary descriptive summary.”
- “Prospective power analysis indicated that approximately 14,745 observations per group would be required to detect a +1.0-point effect at 80% power under the stated planning assumptions.”
- “All public experiment units and outcomes are synthetic.”

## UNSUPPORTED_OR_PROHIBITED_CLAIMS

- “The new interface increased conversion.”
- “The treatment was better.”
- “The experiment proved no effect.”
- “Control and treatment are equal.”
- “The treatment increased conversion by 9.07%.”
- “The business should deploy the variant.”
- “5,000 users was enough.”
- “This analysis used real customer behavior.”
- Any claim that the historical course data are publicly redistributable or that historical assignment was verified as randomized.

Public descriptions must preserve the synthetic framing, treat relative lift as secondary, distinguish statistical from practical significance, and avoid company, causal, deployment, revenue, or ROI claims.
