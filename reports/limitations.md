# Limitations

- This is a statistical demonstration, not a deployed website experiment and not evidence about actual customers.
- Historical dataset creator, source, license, redistribution rights, live-versus-synthetic status, assignment, exposure, duration, and stopping process remain unresolved. Historical rows are not used publicly.
- Synthetic results depend on declared probabilities and an idealized generator. They demonstrate method, not external validity.
- Causal interpretation is confined to the controlled synthetic assignment and generation assumptions; it does not transfer to the historical course data or a real organization.
- The +1.0 percentage-point practical threshold is a `PORTFOLIO_SCENARIO_ASSUMPTION`, not a validated business threshold. No revenue, ROI, cost, or deployment value is estimated.
- The primary outcome is one binary conversion measure. Guardrails, engagement metrics, delayed outcomes, attrition, noncompliance, interference, and instrumentation failures are outside scope.
- One row per unit eliminates clustering and repeated-user complexity by design. The demonstration therefore does not show clustered or longitudinal inference.
- No subgroup analysis is performed. The project does not make segment-specific claims.
- The fixed-horizon analysis would not be valid under repeated unplanned peeking and significance-based stopping. No sequential design is implemented.
- Prospective power uses a standard large-sample two-proportion approximation. It is suitable for planning here but remains a scenario-based calculation.
- The alternative Wald interval is a sensitivity check only; the Newcombe interval is primary.
- Bayesian analysis is omitted intentionally. Post-hoc observed power is not used.
